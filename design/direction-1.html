Visual Direction 01

Editorial Noir

Stark monochrome inspired by Chanel's runway minimalism and Apple's whitespace discipline. A single warm gold accent — the only chromatic note in a sea of ivory and black. Cormorant Garamond lends editorial gravitas; Inter keeps the UI precise. Every surface breathes.
