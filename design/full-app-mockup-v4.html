Trésor — the full application

Sixteen screens presented as catalogue plates — three skippable arrival plates now open the house before any ask. Onboarding follows Nigel's phone-first architecture; every pattern below is drawn from the sixteen-source research file — UAE phone conventions, OTP behaviour, WhatsApp delivery, and the editorial restraint of Net-a-Porter and SSENSE.
