Identity Refinement · v8

Two Marks, Refined

The Vault Arch and the Vault Door, carried forward unchanged in direction and recut with finished drafting — three proofs of each, both colorways, hero and favicon scale.

What this sheet is. Concept A — The Vault Arch — from logo-concepts.html, and Variation 02 — The Vault Door — from logo-vault-arch-variations.html, and nothing else. The concepts are untouched; only the execution is refined. In the working proofs the arches sprang from staggered heights, the echo gaps drifted, and the door's seam floated above its own crown. Here every mark is drawn from one center: shared springlines, constant concentric reveals, sill overhangs equal to the reveal gap, seams seated exactly at the door-face crown. The three cuts of each mark differ only in stance and weight — proportional decisions, not new ideas.

The Vault Door

What Changed

The Drafting Rules

Nothing in the two concepts moved — the arch is still one unbroken line with an echo and a sill, the door is still three arches, a Vermeil face, a seam. What changed is that every mark on this sheet now obeys the same four rules, which the working proofs broke in small ways that read as unfinished:

  • One center. Every arch in a mark springs from the same springline and shares one center point, so concentric lines are truly concentric — the reveals no longer pinch at the crown.
  • One reveal. The gap between lines is a single number per cut (7, 6.5, 8, 6…), and the sill's overhang past each jamb is that same number. One measurement governs each drawing.
  • Seated seams. The vault door's seam begins exactly at the door-face crown, not at an arbitrary height above it.
  • Honest favicons. At 32px each mark keeps only what survives: the arch drops its echo, the door drops its depth arch and seam, and every remaining stroke thickens to hold roughly one rendered pixel.

Recommended pairings if one of each advances: The Portrait for the arch — its golden stance gives the concept the human scale the original critique asked for — and The Section for the door, the cut that most reads as a single object. The Ironwork is the strongest pure favicon on the sheet if the mark must ever stand alone at 32px.
