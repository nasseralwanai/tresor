## Description

Tresor is a private, invitation-only platform for discerning collectors of luxury goods. Catalog your bags, watches, jewelry, and accessories with full provenance tracking. Share your collection exclusively with trusted circle members. Lend pieces with confidence, track borrows, and maintain a complete ownership history.

**Catalog Your Collection**
Document every piece with brand, model, condition, and serial number. Upload multiple photos per item. Track purchase details, estimated values, and authenticity verification — all kept private by default. Your financial information is never shared with circle members.

**Private Circles**
Create or join an exclusive circle of trusted collectors. Only members you invite can see your shared items. Every interaction stays within your circle — no public profiles, no strangers, no exposure.

**Lend with Confidence**
Track who has borrowed your pieces and for how long. Receive gentle reminders when items are out. Record offline borrows for in-person handovers. Maintain a complete lending history for every item.

**Co-Ownership**
Split ownership of shared pieces with transparent share tracking. Record contributions and maintain a clear ownership ledger for co-invested items.

**Activity Feed**
Stay connected with your circle through a curated activity feed. See new additions, borrow activity, and shared wishes. React and comment without exposing your collection to the public.

**Privacy First**
Your collection data is yours alone. Prices and purchase details are visible only to you and co-owners. Circle members see what you choose to share. No data is sold. No analytics trackers. No public visibility.

Tresor is built for those who value discretion as much as they value their collection.
